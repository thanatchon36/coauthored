# 150-word abstract (draft)

Coding agents sign their work with co-author trailers, agent accounts and verbatim markers. Using a
pre-registered design and a catalogue of 52 signatures for 28 tools, each backed by public example commits, we
measured the share of AI-attributed commits from 2023 to 2026 in 42 open-source repositories that train and
serve AI models, 12 coding-agent harnesses and 42 matched popular non-AI repositories, reading 2.85 million
commits on one day. In July–September 2026, 8.7% of commits to AI infrastructure were AI-attributed (95% CI
4.0–14.5%), up from almost none in 2024. Matched controls reached 7.1% and the harnesses 10.3%; the intervals
cannot tell the groups apart. Of the hypotheses about the curve, only the level one met its pre-registered rule.
Attribution survived the merge in 92% of sampled pull requests. All shares are lower bounds: attribution can be
disabled, stripped, lost in exports or never added. Code, aggregates and paper are open.
